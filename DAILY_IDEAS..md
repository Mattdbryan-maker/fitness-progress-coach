#### Exercise Knowledge Base & AI Coaching ####

## Vision

Create a comprehensive exercise knowledge base that goes beyond simply storing exercise names. The goal is to build an educational platform that teaches users how to perform exercises correctly while providing personalised AI coaching based on their training data.

---

## Exercise Library

Instead of manually typing exercise names, users can select exercises from a built-in library.

### Benefits

- Faster workout logging.
- Consistent exercise names.
- Prevents spelling mistakes.
- Better data analysis.
- More accurate AI recommendations.
- Easier workout planning.

---

## Exercise Information

Each exercise should include:

- Exercise name
- Category (Push, Pull, Legs, Core, Cardio, etc.)
- Primary muscles worked
- Secondary muscles worked
- Equipment required
- Difficulty level
- Exercise description
- Step-by-step instructions
- Coaching cues
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Safety considerations
- Recommended rep ranges
- Progression options
- Regression options
- Alternative exercises
- Images or video demonstrations (future feature)

---

## Advanced Exercise Library

Unlike many fitness apps, the library should include both beginner and advanced exercises.

Examples include:

- Bayesian Cable Curl
- JM Press
- Pendulum Squat
- Hatfield Squat
- Meadows Row
- Larsen Press
- Seal Row
- Spider Curl
- Jefferson Curl (with appropriate safety guidance)
- Deficit Romanian Deadlift

The aim is to create one of the most complete exercise libraries available.

---

## Workout Templates

Allow users to select complete workouts instead of adding every exercise individually.

Examples:

- Push
- Pull
- Legs
- Upper
- Lower
- Full Body
- Custom

Selecting a template automatically loads the exercises, allowing the user to simply enter:

- Weight
- Sets
- Reps

---

## AI Coaching

Use the user's workout history alongside the exercise knowledge base to provide personalised coaching.

Potential AI features include:

- Exercise recommendations
- Alternative exercise suggestions
- Progressive overload guidance
- Plateau detection
- Weekly training summaries
- Recovery recommendations
- Training volume analysis
- Muscle group balance analysis
- Workout feedback
- Technique reminders
- Personalised programme generation

The AI should explain *why* it is making each recommendation rather than simply telling the user what to do.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Build more than just a workout tracker.

Create an intelligent fitness platform that combines:

- Workout tracking
- Exercise education
- Scientific training principles
- Personal coaching
- AI-powered recommendations

The aim is for the application to become a knowledgeable training partner that helps users improve safely and effectively rather than simply recording workouts.

##### Workout Sessions ####

## Vision

Store all exercises completed during a workout inside a single workout session, allowing users to view and analyse each gym session as a whole rather than as individual exercises.

---

## Concept

Instead of saving every exercise as a completely separate workout, each training session should contain a collection of exercise dictionaries.

Example:

Workout Session
├── Date: 30/07/2026
├── Chest & Triceps
│
├── Bench Press
├── Incline Dumbbell Press
├── Cable Fly
├── Tricep Pushdown
└── Overhead Extension

---

## Benefits

- View previous workouts exactly as they were completed.
- Makes browsing workout history much easier.
- Allows users to repeat previous sessions.
- Enables comparison between sessions over time.
- Creates a more natural gym experience, as users train in sessions rather than isolated exercises.

---

## Future AI Opportunities

Using complete workout sessions will allow the AI to:

- Analyse overall training volume.
- Detect progression across entire workouts.
- Identify missing muscle groups.
- Recommend improvements to workout structure.
- Suggest changes based on previous sessions.
- Generate weekly and monthly training summaries.

# What Makes This App Different?

Many fitness apps focus on recording workouts.

This application aims to help users understand *why* they are training a certain way and *how* to improve over time.

The long-term vision is to combine structured workout tracking, a comprehensive exercise knowledge base, and intelligent AI coaching into one platform that genuinely helps people become stronger, healthier, and more confident in the gym.
